Privacy Policy

SefyStudio Publisher · Last updated: October 20, 2026 · Developer: SefyStudio · Contact: support@sefystudio.com

SefyStudio Publisher is an internal tool run by SefyStudio. It posts SefyStudio’s own videos and posts to SefyStudio’s own accounts on YouTube, Instagram and X. It has no public users, no sign-up and no other customers.

What the tool accesses

Only the SefyStudio accounts that the studio owner has connected. For YouTube, the tool uses the youtube.upload permission to upload videos to SefyStudio’s own channel. For Instagram and X, it uses the permissions needed to read basic account information and publish SefyStudio’s own content.

What we store

Access tokens for these accounts are kept as encrypted environment secrets in our own production environment. They are never shared, sold or written into code, logs or public files. Videos waiting to be published are kept briefly in our own cloud storage and deleted after publishing.

What we do not do

We do not collect personal data about anyone else, and we do not sell or share data with third parties. We do not use data from Google APIs for advertising.

Google API Services

SefyStudio Publisher’s use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

SefyStudio Publisher uses YouTube API Services. By using it you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service; see the Google Privacy Policy for how Google handles data.

Removing access

Access can be withdrawn at any time from the connected account’s security settings (for Google: myaccount.google.com/permissions). Stored tokens are then deleted from our environment.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date above.
