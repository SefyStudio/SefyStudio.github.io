The game

Tap, match & weave

Easy to learn, endlessly satisfying: every yarn you sort knits its share of a picture on the loom.

Sort the yarn

Pick yarn balls from the pile and send them to the right colour. Soft sounds and a gentle tick when a row is done.

Collect 200+ patterns

Fill a sticker album across eight collections, from Kilim Classics and Sweet Shop to Sea Life and Adventure.

Happy customers

Weave each order before your customer loses patience, and meet a new customer of the day.

A little calm in every level

Grow your yarn shop, decorate the Cozy Corner and the Loom Workshop, keep a win streak going and open a daily gift. Play anywhere, no Wi-Fi needed.
