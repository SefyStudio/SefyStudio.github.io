Loom Pop!

Yarn Ball Shooter

Shoot yarn balls into the rolling chain, match three of a colour and watch every pop race round the loom and knit a cozy picture, stitch by stitch.

Download on theApp Store

How it plays

Aim, pop, weave

Match three yarn balls of a colour. Each pop sends its yarn to the loom and the picture grows.

Fever combos

Chain reactions pull the yarn back and pay out coins. Six in a row and Fever starts.

200 patterns

Eight cozy collections, from Kilim Classics to Sea Life, all in your pattern book.
