# SefyStudio site

Live at https://sefystudio.com (GitHub Pages, branch `main`, CNAME `sefystudio.com`).

- Look: the cozy SefyStudio theme (`assets/site.css`). Only DealCraft's wide promo card (home top, game card, top of `/dealcraft/`) uses DealCraft's "Masa" look, scoped to `.dc-*`.
- `/` studio home · `/dealcraft/` (EN) and `/dealcraft/tr/` (TR) with privacy, support and terms pages (App Store links for DealCraft).
- `/loom-pop/`, `/mix-bar/` and the unlisted `/pip-and-pals/` support, privacy and join pages are linked from those apps: keep every URL working. DealCraft's legal texts are in `_dealcraft.py`; `_legacy.py` is the previous builder, kept for reference.
- All pages are generated by `python3 _build.py` (one template). Edit `_build.py`, not the HTML.
- Keep `app-ads.txt`, `robots.txt` and `CNAME` at the root.
