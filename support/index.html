Spirit Fly Support

Help with gameplay, advertising, Game Center, or privacy.

Before contacting us

  • Update Spirit Fly and iOS to the latest available versions.
  • Close and reopen the Game.
  • Check your internet connection for rewarded ads and Game Center.
  • Confirm that you are signed into Game Center in iOS Settings.

Contact

Public support contact details will be added here before Spirit Fly is released on the App Store.

When contacting support, include your device model, iOS version, Game version, and a short description of what happened. Never send your Apple ID password, payment details, or advertising identifiers.

Privacy requests

Once the public contact address is available, begin the subject line with Spirit Fly Privacy Request. We may need limited information to understand and verify a request, but we will never ask for a password.
