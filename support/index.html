Spirit Fly Support

Help with gameplay, advertising, Game Center, or privacy.

Before contacting us

  • Update Spirit Fly and iOS to the latest available versions.
  • Close and reopen the Game.
  • Check your internet connection for rewarded ads and Game Center.
  • Confirm that you are signed into Game Center in iOS Settings.

Contact support

Open a Spirit Fly support request and describe the issue. Include your device model, iOS version, Game version, and the steps that led to the problem.

Support requests are public. Never include passwords, payment details, advertising identifiers, precise location, or other sensitive personal information.

Privacy requests

For a privacy question, open a support request with the title Spirit Fly Privacy Request. Do not post identity documents or sensitive information. If private information is needed to handle the request, state that in the request and SefyStudio will provide an appropriate private follow-up method.
