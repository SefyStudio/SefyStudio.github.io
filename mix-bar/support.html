Support

Something not working, an idea, or a level you love? Email support@sefystudio.com (please add your iPhone model and the level number). We answer within a few days.

How do I play?

Tap a glass, then tap another glass to pour. A glass full of one flavour goes to the customer who ordered it. Pour Cherry onto Lemonade for Orange, Lemonade onto Blue Fizz for Kiwi, Cherry onto Blue Fizz for Grape. Milk and Cola never mix. Drinks mix only when you pour.

“Wrong mix!”, what now?

When a level can no longer be finished, the bar at the bottom offers Rewind (back three pours), Undo, or a free Restart.

I bought something but did not get it

Open the Shop and tap “Restore Purchases” at the bottom. Coin packs are delivered once per purchase; No Ads and the Starter Pack restore on every device with the same Apple Account.

My progress on a new phone

Turn on iCloud for your Apple Account before you open the game on the new phone; your levels, stars and bar come back automatically.

Change my ad privacy choices

In the EEA, UK and Switzerland, open Settings in the game and tap “Privacy Choices”. Tracking permission can be changed in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

Request a refund

Purchases are handled by Apple: visit reportaproblem.apple.com.
