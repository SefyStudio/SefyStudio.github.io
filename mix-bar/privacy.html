Privacy Policy

Mix Bar: Water Sort Puzzle · Last updated: October 18, 2026 · Developer: SefyStudio · Contact: support@sefystudio.com

The short version

There are no accounts and no sign-up. We do not run a user database. Your progress stays on your device (and in your own iCloud, if it is on). The free version shows ads through Google AdMob.

What stays on your device

Your progress (levels, stars, coins, boosters, your bar, settings) is stored on your device. We cannot see it. Deleting the app deletes it.

iCloud backup

If iCloud is on for your Apple Account, the game keeps the same progress in your private iCloud storage (iCloud key-value storage) so a new phone can continue where you left off. This data is stored by Apple under your Apple Account and governed by Apple’s privacy policy. We cannot access it.

Anonymous game statistics

To balance the levels we may count anonymous game events (for example: a level was started, won or lost, a booster or ad was used, a purchase was made). They carry a random number made on your device, never your name, email, advertising ID or location, and they are not used for advertising or shared with advertisers. They are processed by TelemetryDeck, a privacy-focused analytics service in the EU.

Ads (Google AdMob)

The game shows rewarded videos (only when you choose to watch one) and occasional full-screen ads between levels, served by Google AdMob. Google and its partners may collect and use your device’s advertising identifier (IDFA), IP address, approximate (coarse) location, ad interaction data and diagnostic data (such as crash and performance data) to deliver, measure and, with your permission, personalize ads and to prevent fraud. See how Google uses information from apps that use its services and Google’s Privacy Policy.

Consent (EEA, UK, Switzerland). Where required, a consent form from Google’s User Messaging Platform (UMP) asks for your choices before ads load. You can change them at any time in the game: Settings → Privacy Choices.

Tracking permission (App Tracking Transparency). iOS asks whether the app may track you across other companies’ apps and websites. If you decline, the advertising identifier is not shared and you see non-personalized ads. You can change this in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

Purchases

Coin packs, the Starter Pack and No Ads are bought through the Apple App Store (StoreKit). We never see your payment details or your Apple Account. The game only learns which product was bought, so it can deliver it.

Game Center and reminders

If you are signed in to Game Center, the game reports achievements and scores to Apple’s Game Center (Apple’s privacy policy applies). Reminders are local notifications scheduled on your device; nothing is sent to a server.

Children

Mix Bar is a general-audience game and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has given us personal information, contact us and we will help remove it.

Your rights

Because we do not keep personal data that identifies you, there is usually nothing for us to export or delete. To reset your progress, delete the app (and, if you want, its iCloud data in iOS Settings → [your name] → iCloud → Manage Storage). For ad data held by Google, use your ad choices above or Google’s privacy tools. For any privacy question or request, email support@sefystudio.com.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date above.
