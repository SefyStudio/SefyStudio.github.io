Mix Bar

Water Sort Puzzle

Tap a glass, then another to pour. Flavours mix when you pour them: cherry on lemonade makes orange. Fill a glass with one flavour and serve the customer who ordered it.

Coming soon to theApp Store

How it plays

Sort and mix

Pour layer onto layer. Some flavours mix into new drinks, milk and cola never do.

Plan every pour

Every level has a move limit. No timers, just clever moves, and every level is solvable.

Build your bar

Stars renovate the diner, then open a beach bar, an ice cream parlor and a rooftop lounge.
