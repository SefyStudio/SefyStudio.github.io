Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 31, 2026 · Last updated: July 31, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how SefyStudio ("we," "us," or "our") handles information when you use Spirit Fly (the "Game").

1. Information handled by the Game

Spirit Fly does not require a SefyStudio account. Gameplay settings, progress, and similar preferences may be stored locally on your device. We do not operate a separate server database for player profiles.

The Game uses third-party services that may process information as described below:

  • Google AdMob: provides optional rewarded advertisements. Depending on your device, region, settings, and consent choices, Google may process information such as IP address, device and advertising identifiers, ad interactions, diagnostics, performance data, and general device information.
  • Google User Messaging Platform (UMP): requests and records privacy choices where required and provides access to privacy options.
  • Apple Game Center: provides authentication and leaderboards. Apple may process your Game Center player identifier, display name, scores, and related gameplay information under your Apple account settings.
  • Apple and app distribution services: may process purchase-free download, diagnostics, crash, and device information as part of distributing and operating the Game.

2. How information is used

Information handled through these services may be used to:

  • operate, secure, troubleshoot, and improve the Game;
  • load and measure optional rewarded advertisements;
  • prevent fraud, abuse, and invalid advertising activity;
  • provide Game Center authentication and leaderboards;
  • remember and honor your privacy choices; and
  • comply with legal obligations.

3. Rewarded advertising and consent

Rewarded ads are optional. The Game explains the in-game reward before you choose to watch an ad. If you do not complete the ad, the stated reward may not be granted.

Where required, the Game asks for your privacy choices before requesting ads. Available choices may include personalized or non-personalized advertising, depending on your region and the options offered by Google. You can reopen privacy choices from the Game's privacy options entry point when it is required or available.

4. Legal bases and regional rights

Where the European Economic Area, United Kingdom, or Swiss data protection laws apply, processing may rely on your consent, our legitimate interests in operating and securing the Game, performance of a service you request, or compliance with law. You may withdraw consent through the available privacy options. Withdrawal does not affect processing that occurred before withdrawal.

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to request access, correction, deletion, restriction, portability, or objection, and to appeal or opt out of certain targeted advertising, sale, or sharing of personal information. SefyStudio does not independently sell personal information. Advertising partners may process data for advertising subject to your choices and applicable law.

5. Data sharing

Information may be processed by Google, Apple, their service providers, and authorities where required by law. We do not rent or sell player contact details. Third-party providers handle information under their own privacy policies and terms:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses information for advertising
  • Apple Privacy Policy

6. Retention and security

Locally stored gameplay information remains on your device until you clear it, uninstall the Game, or the operating system removes it. Third-party providers determine retention periods for information they process. We use reasonable measures intended to protect the Game, but no electronic system is completely secure.

7. International transfers

Google and Apple may process information in countries other than your own. Their privacy notices explain the safeguards they use for international transfers.

8. Children's privacy

Spirit Fly is not directed to children under 13, or the minimum age required to consent to data processing in their country. We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us so we can review the request.

9. Your device controls

You can manage Game Center through Apple settings, limit advertising permissions through device settings, and change available consent choices through the Game's privacy options. You may also delete locally stored Game data by uninstalling the Game, subject to device backup behavior.

10. Changes to this Policy

We may update this Policy when the Game, third-party services, or legal requirements change. The latest version will appear on this page with a revised date.

11. Contact

For privacy questions or requests, use the contact method on our Support page. Please do not include passwords, payment details, or other sensitive information.
