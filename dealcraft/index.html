Questions

Is it free?

Retail (30 chapters) is free. The other sectors, the Sales Marathon and ad-free play come with a one-time full-version purchase. No subscription.

Do I need an account?

No. Your progress stays on your device. The leaderboard is optional and only asks for a display name.

Does it need internet?

No, it plays offline too. For the leaderboard, chapters played online count.

Is it real sales training?

DealCraft is a training game. Legal and technical details in the chapters are simplified; they are not legal or financial advice.
