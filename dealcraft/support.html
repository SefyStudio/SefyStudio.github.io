Support

Something not working, an idea, or a customer you loved? Email support@sefystudio.com with your iPhone model, iOS version and, if it's about a chapter, its sector and number. A person who works on the game answers, usually within a day or two.

How do I play?

A customer sits across the table. Read their line, then tap one of the reply cards before the clock runs out. The best card uses the right sales technique for this customer at this moment; some cards are traps that sound right. After each move you see why it worked or not. Win the customer over and close the deal to finish the chapter. The ? button on the home screen opens the full guide.

Answer by voice doesn't work

Voice answers need microphone and speech recognition permission. Turn them on in iOS Settings → DealCraft. You can always tap the cards instead.

Get the full version / restore my purchase

Tap any locked sector, or Full version / restore purchase at the bottom of the home screen. Use Restore purchase with the Apple ID you bought it with to unlock it on a new iPhone.

Refunds

Purchases are handled by Apple: visit reportaproblem.apple.com.

Ads and privacy choices

In the EEA, UK and Switzerland, tap Ad choices at the bottom of the home screen. Tracking permission can be changed in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. The full version has no ads.

Leaderboard

Joining is optional and only needs a display name (and a company code if your company gave you one). To report a name, tap Report next to it: it is hidden on your device at once and reviewed. To leave and delete your data, tap Leave the board and delete my data.

Company boards

Want a private board for your sales team? Email us with your company name and we'll send an invite code.

My progress is gone

Progress is saved on your device. Deleting the app or clearing its data removes it. Your full-version purchase is never lost: restore it with the same Apple ID.
