Privacy Policy

DealCraft · Last updated: November 16, 2026 · Developer and data controller: SefyStudio · Contact: support@sefystudio.com

The short version

You don't need an account, an email address or a phone number to play. Your progress stays on your iPhone. If you choose to join the optional leaderboard, we store the display name you pick and your chapter scores, and you can delete them in the app at any time. Players without the full version see ads from Google AdMob. Payments are handled by Apple.

1. Data that stays on your device

Chapter progress, stars, medals, achievements, statistics for your technique report, and your language, sound and practice settings are stored only on your device. We cannot see them. Deleting the app deletes them.

2. Online play

When you are online, a chapter is played on our game server (Cloudflare Workers). The server sends the customer's lines and cards and keeps the game state in an encrypted token that your app holds; it does not ask for or store personal data. To stop the same finished game from being counted twice, we keep a one-way hash of finished game tokens. Like any website, our hosting provider Cloudflare, Inc. briefly processes your IP address to deliver the service and to protect it from abuse. Offline, chapters are played on your device and nothing is sent.

3. Leaderboard (optional)

If you join the leaderboard we process:

  • the display name you choose;
  • a random player id and a secret key created on your device (we store only a hash of the key);
  • the score and stars of chapters you finish online, with the time they were recorded.

Why: to show the leaderboard you asked to join. Legal basis: performance of the service you requested (GDPR Art. 6(1)(b); KVKK Art. 5(2)(c)). Where: Cloudflare's database (Cloudflare D1). How long: until you delete it.

Delete: in the app, open Leaderboard → Leave the board and delete my data. Your name, scores and reports are deleted from our server immediately. You can also email us.

Display names and reports. Names are checked against a list of offensive words. Any player can report a name; a report stores the reported player id, the reporter's player id (or, for viewers who have not joined, a one-way hash of their IP address) and the time. A name reported by two different players is hidden. Reports are deleted with the reported or reporting player's data.

4. Ads (Google AdMob)

Players without the full version see a rewarded video only when they choose to watch one (to remove a wrong card) and an occasional full-screen ad between chapters, never during a chapter. Ads are served by Google AdMob (Google Ireland Limited / Google LLC). Google may collect and use your device's advertising identifier (IDFA), IP address, approximate location, ad interactions and diagnostic data to deliver, measure and, with your permission, personalise ads and to prevent fraud. See how Google uses information from apps that use its services and Google's Privacy Policy.

Consent (EEA, UK, Switzerland). Before ads load, Google's consent form (User Messaging Platform) asks for your choices. You can change them at any time from the home screen of the game: Ad choices.

Tracking permission. iOS asks whether DealCraft may track you across other companies' apps and websites (App Tracking Transparency). If you decline, the advertising identifier is not shared and ads are not personalised. The game works the same either way. You can change this in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. With the full version, no ads are shown.

5. Purchases (Apple)

The full version is a one-time in-app purchase handled entirely by Apple through the App Store (StoreKit). We never see your payment details or your Apple ID, and we run no purchase server: the game only learns on your device that the full version was bought, so it can unlock it and restore it on your other devices.

6. Game Center

If you are signed in to Game Center, your achievements and seller score are sent to Apple Game Center and shown according to your Game Center settings, under Apple's Privacy Policy. We don't receive your Game Center identity.

7. Voice answers

If you tap Answer by voice, iOS asks for microphone and speech recognition permission. Your speech is turned into text by Apple's speech recognition (on your iPhone when the device supports it, otherwise by Apple's servers under Apple's privacy policy) and the text is used only to pick the matching card on your device. We do not record, store or receive your voice or the text. You can always play by tapping the cards instead.

8. International transfers

Cloudflare and Google may process data outside your country, including in the United States. These providers use safeguards such as the EU Standard Contractual Clauses. For players in Türkiye, data is transferred abroad on the basis of these safeguards and, where required, your explicit consent given through the in-app permission and consent screens (KVKK Art. 9).

9. Children

DealCraft is a general-audience game and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has joined the leaderboard, contact us and we will delete it.

10. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR, the UK GDPR, Türkiye's Law No. 6698 (KVKK) or US state laws such as the CCPA), you can ask to access, correct, delete or receive a copy of your data, object to or restrict processing, and withdraw consent. We do not sell or share personal data for money. Email support@sefystudio.com; we answer within 30 days. You may also complain to your data protection authority (in Türkiye: Kişisel Verileri Koruma Kurumu).

11. Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date above and, for important changes, tell you in the app.
