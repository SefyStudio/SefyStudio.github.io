Pip & Pals Hide & Seek

Three friends fell from their moon. Hide from the drones, finish the tasks and fix the rocket, alone or with friends.

The game

Hide in plain sight

Become a bush, a barrel, even a cow. Stay still while the drones sweep past, then sneak on to the next task.

Morph & hide

Turn into the objects around you and hold your breath. Every world brings a new drone with its own way of hunting.

Play with friends

Team up with up to 3 pals: share a room code, split the tasks and revive each other. Works across phone, tablet and web.

Spotted? Run!

Dash, dodge and shake off the drones, crack secret codes and pull the right levers to fix the rocket home.

See it in motion

21 levels in 7 worlds

From sunny meadows to a moonlit night: clever tasks, secret codes and a rocket to fix. Play the full game right here in your browser, or get the app for iPhone and iPad.
